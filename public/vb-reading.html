9 / 03 / The system · Segment 1

Choose the depth

Match the board’s depth to the decision you need.

  1. ExploreStop when the direction can be compared or discussed
  2. ExplainStop when a reader understands the journey and behaviour
  3. Review for a buildStop when the responsible reviewer can make the outstanding decisions
  4. PresentStop when the requested output has been rendered and inspected

25 / 05 / Examples · Segment 17

Kinfolk Clinics: the brief

Made-up client · Built, not run

Kinfolk Clinics

Four family physiotherapy clinics in Leeds. They want a booking app that replaces phone booking.

  1. Book, move or cancel in under a minute
  2. Choose a clinician by specialism and availability
  3. Reminders and a waiting list that fills cancelled slots
  4. Intake form before the first visit
  5. Front-desk view of the day
Intake answers the board would record
Audience and next decisionPractice manager deciding scope for phase 1
Subject and scopeNew app. Everything is PROPOSED; no existing system to verify
FormatResponsive HTML board; phone-first scenes
BrandProposed until Kinfolk approves: warm sage, chalk white, charcoal; one humanist sans
FidelityCore booking journey detailed; front-desk view simplified
Key statesSlot available, slot taken mid-booking, cancellation, waiting-list offer, intake incomplete, payment declined
Owner and approvalsPractice manager signs phase 1
ConstraintsAccessible for older patients: large targets, readable static mode
