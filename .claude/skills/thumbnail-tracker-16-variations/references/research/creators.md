# The three creators

One section per creator, each with the same headings, so you can design "in the style of" any of them. Method, confounds and the cross-creator laws are in `references/research/overview.md`.

**Before designing in a creator's style, open their contact sheet:** `assets/research/top-hits-nick.jpg`, `assets/research/top-hits-sabri.jpg` or `assets/research/top-hits-riley.jpg`. The words below describe the frames; the sheet shows them.

How to read the figures:
- Single-video ratios are the all-time `all.tsv` figures. Where the latest-30 snapshot gave a different ratio, it follows as "was X". Group medians from the latest-30 study (for example "`time-box` n=5, 4.68") are quoted as computed then.
- Latest-30 dates run 29 May to 10 Oct 2026. Ratios for anything under about 3 weeks old are provisional.
- Titles are verbatim, including the creators' own spelling and capitals.

### "In this creator's style" recipe

- **Title:** first person, a searchable tool noun plus a stake or verdict, about 54 characters. Pick from `references/frameworks/headline-framework.md` §2: I tested, What I'd do instead, Contrarian verdict, Full course, Cheap result. - **Title:** the product name in the first two words, then a stake, a count or a verdict, with one parenthetical payoff. Prefer a challenge ("We Built…"), a beginner course, or a single-launch verdict over "X Just Changed". ALL-CAPS intensifiers only if the user's channel already speaks that way.
- **Thumbnail:** dark background, orange accent. Two faces at the edges with a lowercase verdict in the centre for collaborations; otherwise a solo face on the right with about 4 words and one orange-pill word. For weekly news, the desk anchor is acceptable, but always with a verdict line.
- **Description:** `references/frameworks/description-framework.md` "Riley style": the story first (series label lower), a bullet recap of the 3–5 biggest moments, the resources link, numbered chapters, a comment prompt. Partner tags only when required.
- **Renderer archetypes (`references/design/playbook.md` §3):** `versus` for the duo; `verdict` for a launch take with two rival logos; `command` for a single feature or UI; `syllabus` for beginner courses; `equation` for a logo → logo card. The desk anchor and mascot arena have no archetype; build the scene with `references/design/image-prompts.md`.
