# Quality gates and honest reporting

Passing the scripts' checks doesn't make a thumbnail good. The checks catch broken rules; looking at the images catches bad design. Do both, and report which you did.

## Hard gates (never deliver without these)

- **Proof:** every number, result or quote on a thumbnail, title, description or post is said in the video or supplied by the user. Nothing invented. No client names unless cleared.
- **People:** faces are only the user's own or ones they have rights to. No real third party's posts or likeness. Logos never imply an endorsement.
- **Files:** each file is the right size and format for its platform (`references/platforms.md`), under 2MB, and labelled with platform and size.
- **Renderer:** no unexplained `WARNING` lines (missing logos or assets).
- **Packager:** no unexplained `CHECK:` or `SCHEMA:` lines. Every `NOTE:` placeholder is listed for the user to fill, or filled from known links.
- **Description:** chapters from 0:00, ascending, at least 3, ≥10 seconds apart, from real timestamps. A disclosure next to any affiliate or sponsor link.
- **Brand:** house style applied when the brand is Pecuna Factorem (`references/brand/house-style.md`).

## Visual review (look at every image)

Use `references/frameworks/thumbnail-framework.md` §7:
- readable at 168px;
- squint and greyscale tests;
- timestamp corner and play-button zone;
- collisions;
- dead zones;
- distinct from recent uploads.

Look at `_feed-preview.png` every time. Fix and re-render before showing anything.

## Scores are rankings, not predictions

The title score (0–100) and the thumbnail scorecard (0–100) rank a batch. Neither predicts views. Say so when giving them.

## Reporting status

- Separate "the data shows" from "my read". Give n and confidence for research claims (`references/research/overview.md`).
- Say when a rule is a house rule or a brand rule rather than research. For example, 3–5 hashtags is a brand rule, while all three creators barely use hashtags.
- Say when something wasn't verified: a third-party size, an untested LinkedIn rule, a render only checked as stills.
- Never claim a thumbnail or title "will" perform. Recommend the A/B test and the 48-hour CTR check (`references/learning-loop.md`).
