# Whiteboard Wednesdays weekly process

Agreed with Taylor on 16 October 2026, with posting days confirmed on 24 October. Updated on 11 November 2026 (Taylor: "Recording days are going to be Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Posting days are going to be Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday."). This replaces every earlier schedule.

Since 10 November 2026 the posting days come from Posting settings (the settings cog, `#/settings/posting`). Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday below are the default, used until Taylor saves other days; the Schedule, the Topics board and the library wall all follow the saved setting. Recording days are Monday, Wednesday and Friday (fixed: `WWStore.posting.RECORD_DAYS` in `public/ww-store.js`).

| Day | Job |
|---|---|
| Monday | Plan all of this week's topics and build the three presentations (slides). Finish last week's videos so they are fully edited. Recording day (video 1) |
| Tuesday | Post day (last Wednesday's video), plus editing |
| Wednesday | Recording day (video 2) |
| Thursday | Post day (last Friday's video), plus editing |
| Friday | Recording day (video 3) |
| Saturday | Post day (this Monday's video) |

## How one video moves through the weeks

Plan and slides (Monday) → Record (Monday, Wednesday or Friday) → Edit → Post

With the default editing gap of 5 days:

- Video 1: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Monday, edited Tuesday, posted that **Saturday**.
- Video 2: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Wednesday, edited, posted the next **Tuesday**.
- Video 3: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Friday, edited, posted the next **Thursday**.

Confirmed by Taylor on 24 October 2026, with the week of 26 October:
- Grok Voice 3.0 (recorded Wed 21 Oct) posts Tue 27 Oct.
- How Good Is Grok Bot Really? (recorded Fri 23 Oct) posts Thu 29 Oct.

## For the Topics planner

- Three recording slots a week: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, one topic each.
- A topic's planning day, editing days and suggested post day follow from its recording day. The post day is the next posting day (Posting settings; default Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday) at least the editing gap (default 5 days) after recording, one video per posting day, earlier recording first. A post date set by hand on a topic always wins.
- Statuses: New → Shortlisted → Planned → Slides ready → Recorded → Edited → Posted.
- Subtopics (a title and a note each) are prepared on Monday with the slides.
