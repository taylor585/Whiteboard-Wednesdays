# Whiteboard Wednesdays weekly process

Agreed with Taylor on 13 October 2026. This replaces every earlier schedule.

| Day | Job |
|---|---|
| Monday | Video editing: edit the two videos recorded the week before |
| Tuesday | Slides: get the Whiteboard Wednesday slides ready for both this Wednesday's and this Friday's recordings. Posting day |
| Wednesday | Recording day |
| Thursday | Posting day |
| Friday | Recording day |

## How one video moves through the week

Slides (Tuesday) → Record (Wednesday or Friday) → Edit (next Monday) → Post (next Tuesday or Thursday)

- A video recorded on Wednesday is edited the following Monday and posted that Tuesday.
- A video recorded on Friday is edited the following Monday and posted that Thursday.

Examples:
- Slides Tue 13 Oct, recorded Wed 14 Oct, edited Mon 19 Oct, posted Tue 20 Oct.
- Slides Tue 13 Oct, recorded Fri 16 Oct, edited Mon 19 Oct, posted Thu 22 Oct.

## For the Topics planner

- Two recording slots a week: Wednesday and Friday, one topic each.
- A topic's slides day, edit day and post day follow from its recording day.
- Statuses: New → Shortlisted → Slides ready → Recorded → Edited → Posted.
- Subtopics (a title and a note each) are prepared on Tuesday with the slides.
