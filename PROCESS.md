# Whiteboard Wednesdays weekly process

Agreed with Taylor on 16 October 2026, with posting days confirmed on 24 October. This replaces every earlier schedule.

Since 10 November 2026 the posting days come from Posting settings (the settings cog, `#/settings/posting`). Tuesday and Thursday below are the default, used until Taylor saves other days; the Schedule, the Topics board and the library wall all follow the saved setting. Recording days stay Wednesday and Friday.

| Day | Job |
|---|---|
| Monday | Plan all of this week's topics. Finish last week's videos so they are fully edited. Build the two presentations (slides) for this week's recordings. |
| Tuesday | Post day (last Wednesday's video), plus editing |
| Wednesday | Recording day (video 1) |
| Thursday | Post day (last Friday's video), plus editing |
| Friday | Recording day (video 2) |

## How one video moves through the weeks

Plan and slides (Monday) → Record (Wednesday or Friday) → Edit → Post the following week

- Video 1: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Wednesday, edited, posted the next **Tuesday**.
- Video 2: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Friday, edited, posted the next **Thursday**.

Confirmed by Taylor on 24 October 2026, with the week of 26 October:

## For the Topics planner

- Two recording slots a week: Wednesday and Friday, one topic each.
- A topic's planning day, editing days and suggested post day follow from its recording day. The post day is the next posting day (Posting settings; default Tuesday and Thursday) at least the editing gap (default 5 days) after recording, one video per posting day, earlier recording first. A post date set by hand on a topic always wins.
- Statuses: New → Shortlisted → Planned → Slides ready → Recorded → Edited → Posted.
- Subtopics (a title and a note each) are prepared on Monday with the slides.
