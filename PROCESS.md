# Whiteboard Wednesdays weekly process

Agreed with Taylor on 16 October 2026. This replaces every earlier schedule, including the 13 October one.

| Day | Job |
|---|---|
| Monday | Plan all of this week's topics. Finish last week's videos so they are fully edited. Build the two presentations (slides) for this week's recordings. |
| Tuesday | Post day, plus editing |
| Wednesday | Recording day (video 1) |
| Thursday | Editing day |
| Friday | Recording day (video 2) |

## How one video moves through the weeks

Plan and slides (Monday) → Record (Wednesday or Friday) → Edit (Thursday, Tuesday, finished by the next Monday) → Post (the next Tuesday)

- Video 1: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Wednesday, edited Thursday, fully edited by the next Monday, posted the next Tuesday.
- Video 2: planned and slides built Monday, recorded Friday, edited and finished by the next Monday, posted the next Tuesday.
- So each Tuesday posts both of the previous week's videos. Tuesday's editing time also picks up anything still unfinished.

Examples (week of 19 October):
- Video 1: planned Mon 19 Oct, recorded Wed 21 Oct, edited Thu 22 Oct, finished Mon 26 Oct, posted Tue 27 Oct.
- Video 2: planned Mon 19 Oct, recorded Fri 23 Oct, finished Mon 26 Oct, posted Tue 27 Oct.

## For the Topics planner

- Two recording slots a week: Wednesday and Friday, one topic each.
- A topic's planning day, editing days and suggested post day follow from its recording day.
- Statuses: New → Shortlisted → Planned → Slides ready → Recorded → Edited → Posted.
- Subtopics (a title and a note each) are prepared on Monday with the slides.
