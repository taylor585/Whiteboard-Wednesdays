# Demonstration assets

Show finished, image-led examples with real readable copy. A description of an asset is not the asset. Load the specialist skill whose craft is needed, and say which one was used; never claim a skill that was not loaded. Reusing an approved asset does not require reloading the skill that made it.

| Asset | Specialist skill when creating or materially revising it |
| --- | --- |
| Ad copy, email persuasion, scripts | `breakthrough-advertising-mastery` (then `finishing-touches-copy-and-scripts` for spoken scripts) |
| Landing page, app or funnel example | `fable-website-funnel-design`, and apply its quality gate |
| Generated static or concept image | `llm-image-creator`, with the actual image tool |
| Animated campaign board | `vision-board` |
| Edited recording | `subi-shots` (see `subi-shots-handoff.md`) |

Read the installed specialist skill before using its techniques or explaining its capabilities; these are task-dependent dependencies, not skills to load for every deck. If an essential skill or tool is unavailable, say so and resolve it before calling the output compliant.

## Static and paid ad

- Show the source excerpt, the adapted hook and the finished branded creative, with actual copy where requested.
- Mark proposed creative as illustrative, not a live campaign. No invented performance numbers, endorsements or claims.
- Strong slide forms: `creative`, `t-transform`, `d-morph`, `d-annot`, `d-feed`.

## Landing page, website or app

- Build the example with the design skill, not a generic hero card: a rendered hero with an obvious headline, the service mechanism and the next action, using meaningful imagery and the client's brand.
- Identify the top five objections from the research and order them along the buyer's decision path. Map each to its page section, fear or desire, evidence and conversion action.
- Explain the intended attention flow, and never claim measured eye tracking or maximum conversion without test evidence. `d-tap` shows the mapping well.
- Show desktop and phone variants when explaining responsive design (the `design-gate` pair).
- For an app, show the real state changes, including loading, empty and error states. Label demonstrative controls that do nothing real.

## Email

- Show a plausible complete email: subject, body, sender identity and one clear call to action.
- Derive the problem and service explanation from the evidence and the confirmed offer. Respect the reader's situation (the busy front desk) rather than blaming them.
- On desktop, a left and right body continuation with explicit reading order. Stack it on phones.
- Keep the source visible in a ribbon with the exact extracted words highlighted. Never shrink the email into illegibility.
- Reason from source, to service, to next step, rather than repeating the headline.

## Video script

- Use the copy skill at the requested spoken duration.
- Show the real script or a readable scene sequence, not a promise of one. Keep spoken words separate from timestamps and production notes unless asked.
- A storyboard shows what changes from scene to scene and its persuasive role, with no promise of algorithmic outcomes.

## Explaining the skills behind each asset

After each asset, a method slide covers input, method, decision, output and quality check. It names the exact skill and shows why the method changed the output, not just that it is "advanced".

- **Breakthrough Advertising:**
  - Cover the applicable mass desire, awareness stage, sophistication, amplification and headline strengthening.
  - Cover the seven techniques: Intensification, Identification, Gradualization, Redefinition, Mechanization, Concentration and Camouflage. Check current wording in the skill.
  - Explain the 38 headline-strengthening approaches through a few worked transformations, not 38 items on one slide. Never invent a technique to fill a count.
- **Web and funnel:** objection order, fear and desire mapping, offer clarity, proof, call to action, visual hierarchy and responsive gates. "The ad headline becomes the page opener" alone is not a method.
- **Image creation:**
  - Show the research angle, composition brief, on-image copy and exact logo overlay.
  - Show the iteration and the 200px thumbnail check beside the finished output.
  - Mention development investment only as far as the user's account supports.

## Qualification survey

- When requested, place `How We Eliminate Tyre Kickers` before the nurture email.
- Show all five questions and every option, in this order: eligibility and offer fit, problem severity, commercial capacity, decision authority, timeline and readiness.
- Trace each rule to confirmed ICP and offer criteria.
  - Hard-disqualify the wrong business, never an invented revenue band or unverified price threshold.
  - Route uncertainty and right-company-wrong-role answers to review or nurture as approved.
- Keep it an illustration, separate from live lead capture, booking or enrolment. Mark DQ options clearly, keep answers within that deck, and test the passing, incomplete, review and DQ paths.

## Vision Board Studio

When requested, follow the explanation with a real working board:
- It maps evidence to paid ad, landing page and email, with purposeful scene changes, play, pause, replay and direct scene selection.
- The source stays visible, the current scene is clear, and nothing forces autoplay.
- It pauses or unloads when its slide is hidden.
