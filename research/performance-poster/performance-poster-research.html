How to read this report

  • Source types. Every hard limit is official (the platform's own help pages, developer docs or named staff on record), third-party (vendor studies, trade press) or observed (our own data, or a figure vendors report without a published method).
  • Confidence. Every finding has a confidence tag: HIGH MEDIUM LOW. Sample sizes (n) are given wherever a study publishes them.
  • Correlation, not proof. Almost every large dataset here is a tool vendor's observational data. It shows what goes with good results, not what causes them.
  • Kept separate. Research findings are kept apart from Pecuna's house rules (e.g. the WhatsApp CTA on line 1). Where the research contradicts a house rule, we say so and recommend a change. Taylor decides.
  • Access limits. Instagram and LinkedIn block logged-out access, and YouTube blocked bulk re-fetching on the day. So creator-level Instagram and LinkedIn samples are tiny or empty. Section 7 lists every gap and how to close it.

Five findings that change what we do

1. Instagram now allows only 5 hashtags per post or Reel, down from 30. @creators announced it on 18 Dec 2025. Instagram says hashtags help label content but don't increase reach. HIGH

2. LinkedIn's "3–5 hashtags" house rule is contradicted by 2026 data. In two large median-based studies (566,957 and 516,144 posts), 0–2 hashtags were neutral and 3+ went with lower reach and fewer likes. Recommendation: 1–2, max 3. MEDIUM

3. YouTube only ever shows 3 hashtags, so a 4th or 5th adds nothing visible. Recommendation: 3 (house rule currently 3–5). HIGH

4. A link in the body of a LinkedIn post goes with lower reach (−19% to −36% across three studies). For video promos, upload a native cut and put the YouTube link in the first comment. MEDIUM

5. The YouTube description doesn't decide the click; the title and thumbnail do. Across 150 videos from Nick Saraev, Riley Brown and Sabri Suby, no description feature consistently separated hits from misses. One rule did hold: never open with a sponsor or third-party link; your own CTA on line 1 is fine. MEDIUM

10-minute workflow per video:

  1. Run autocomplete on the topic noun.
  2. Check the Research tab (popularity, content gap).
  3. Compare 2–3 phrasings in Google Trends (YouTube Search; UK and worldwide; past 12 months).
  4. Put the winner in the first 50 characters of the title and the first 40 of the hook.
  5. Pick 3 hashtags (topic, audience, series) and vet each one's results page.

Hashtag research: what's changed and what works

  • The hashtag feed is going. In July 2025 Rishi Jobanputra (LinkedIn's Feed product lead) said "people were not really using it… we are actually in the process of getting rid of it". He said hashtags still help "when people are trying to search for a specific trend or topic", and advised "don't go overboard". HIGH
  • Following hashtags was removed in 2024, along with creator mode and "Talks about" profile hashtags. LinkedIn's old hashtag help page now returns 404. MED–HIGH
  • Reach falls as hashtags rise. Magicpost (566,957 posts, median impressions): 0 → 874 · 1 → 847 · 2 → 825 · 3 → 637 · 5 → 492. LinkPost (516,144 posts, median likes): 1 hashtag +21%, 4–6 hashtags −40%. Hashtag use fell from 67.9% of posts (2022) to 23.2% (2026). MEDIUM
  • One outlier: Metricool (673,658 posts) claims an 85% impressions lift with hashtags, but doesn't define what it compared against. LOW

Recommendation: 1–2 hashtags, max 3; zero is fine. Put them at the end, on their own line, never in the hook. This contradicts the current Pecuna house rule of 3–5. Taylor to decide; we recommend updating it. (Placement is editorial advice; no study measures it.)

Hashtag research

  • Instagram's official position: hashtags are a labelling and search aid, not a reach lever. Instagram's guidance with the cap: "using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience". Avoid #reels, #explore, #viral, #fyp. HIGH
  • Search is keyword-led. Account search matches username and display name (Meta Transparency Centre, Jun 2026). Mosseri says search also reads captions, comments and the video itself (Jul 2026, reported). Google can index public professional posts (since Jul 2025). MEDIUM
  • Correlation in the largest study: posts with at least 1 hashtag had 31.7% fewer views and 33.9% fewer interactions than average (Metricool, 24.4M posts, Jun 2026). This doesn't show hashtags cause the drop, but they clearly don't help reach. MEDIUM
  • Hashtags in the first comment: unclear under the 5-tag cap; sources conflict. Put them in the caption. MEDIUM
  • Checking for banned tags: open the tag's page in the app. No results, or "recent posts hidden", means restricted. Check Settings → Account Status to see whether you can appear in Search.

Recommendation: 3–5 hashtags, at the end of the caption. Mix: 1 branded + 1 broad + 1–3 niche, chosen by relevance, not post count. Put 1–2 search phrases naturally in line 1 and the body, repeat the key phrase in on-screen text, and add the topic to the display name (e.g. "Taylor, AI for Agencies").

06 · BUILD & USE

How Performance Poster will work

You give it a video topic and brief. It returns the full YouTube description (CTA included), the LinkedIn post and the Instagram caption with hashtags, each checked against the platform's limits.

Using it

  1. Give it the brief. The minimum is the topic, the working or final title, the promise, the real proof said in the video, chapters with real timestamps and the content pillar. Optional: a transcript, links and best lines.
  2. Say "post this", or name a platform ("just the YouTube description").
  3. Get back one copy-and-paste box per platform, with character counts, the hashtags chosen (and why), and any [PLACEHOLDER] left to fill.
  4. Paste and publish. After 48 hours, give it CTR, impressions or engagement, and it logs the result against the hook and hashtags.

The brief (template)

TITLE:        I Built an AI Harness So Claude Code Can Run for 24 Hours
PILLAR:       AI agents / Claude Code
PROMISE:      Use AI harnesses to manage more clients and run tasks for longer with fewer mistakes
AUDIENCE:     Agency owners and 1–3 person teams using Claude Code or Codex
PROOF (said): 2 hours, 20% → 80%, 24–36 hours, 58 problems, 11 parts, 9 steps, under an hour
CHAPTERS:     0:00 The bouncing ball … 29:43 Build your own   (real timestamps only)
BEST LINES:   "It's actually a liability because it's cost you tokens."
LINKS:        contact https://pecunafactorem.com/contact · booking [BOOKING LINK]
PLATFORMS:    YouTube, LinkedIn (native cut + carousel), Instagram (Reel)

LinkedIn post

A hook of ≤110 characters (inside the ~140 mobile fold), in Taylor's voice → a short story or teaching body of 900–1,500 characters (up to 2,500 for teaching) → one specific question → 1–2 hashtags on their own line. Format: a native 30–90 s cut, with the YouTube link in the first comment, or a document carousel of the whiteboard frames. No bait such as "comment to agree".

Instagram caption

A first line of ≤60 characters for Reels or ≤125 for feed, with the outcome and keyword → a body of 125–500 characters for Reels, or under 30 words for carousels → one CTA (comment WORD for a DM, send to a colleague, or link in bio) → 3–5 hashtags (1 branded, 1 broad, 1–3 niche) at the end → alt text per slide. It also suggests a display-name keyword and on-screen text for Reels.
