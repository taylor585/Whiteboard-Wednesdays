How to verify:
- **Instagram:** open the tag's page in the app. No results, or "recent posts hidden", means restricted: drop it. Check the top posts are on topic.
- **LinkedIn:** search the tag → Posts → Latest. Check it's in recent use by agency owners or AI builders, with real engagement.
- **YouTube:** open the tag's results page and check it has related videos.

Log every tag used with `scripts/hashtag_log.py add`, then add results after 48 hours.
