This is the packaging brief for the ICP Buyer Research video, kept exactly as given. `references/brief-template.md` turns it into a reusable template.

```text
You're helping with YouTube packaging for a finished video. Give me:
1. 5 thumbnail concepts
2. 10 title options
3. a full YouTube description

Use the thumbnail tracker skill.

THE VIDEO
- Brand: Pecuna Factorem (Taylor Cunningham). UK English.
- Working title: ICP Buyer Research. Length 39:19, 16:9, fully edited with motion graphics.
- Audience: agency owners and media buyers running Meta ads for clients (also business owners running their own).
- Core promise: why Meta ads land the wrong people, and how buyer research fixes it. You find the ideal customer's
  real fears, desires and exact words, then build ads, landing pages, emails and video scripts from them.
- Format: Taylor talks to camera over a screen recording: a live research report, then Claude.

STORY / KEY POINTS (in order)
1. The problem: ads target the wrong people, bring in low-quality leads, and break down at scale. You lose clients.
   You put 6–8 ads in an ad set, only a couple get spend, and even the winners don't land the ICP.
2. Andromeda (Meta's ad engine) now works as a top-down AND horizontal funnel. It pulls people in from the sides at
   any stage, so people already educated elsewhere convert faster. Targeting the right ICP is the whole game.
3. ICP Research Radar: an AI agent Taylor built. It researches the top 3 ICPs, and that becomes the cornerstone for
   paid ads, the landing page, email and video. An audience splits by objection: 40% / 25% / 15% / smaller slices.
4. Live report walkthrough (example client: appointment-based med spas and clinics, fit score 88%):
   - fears, desires, buyer questions and real-life quotes, each with an impact score
   - a real quote: "Someone googles med spa, tries calling, no answer, books at the place next door instead."
5. Belief line: "AI doesn't know how to speak to humans." AI scripts "just sound off" and don't convert. Get scripts
   90% done with AI, then speak every word yourself.
6. Diagnose before you write: trace the argument back from quote → finding → creative decision. Give each asset one
   job ("No answer. Next clinic.").
7. Into Claude with a Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz) skill and the five stages of awareness. Turn a
   buyer's own fear into the opening hook: "Med spa owners, are you fed up of not being able to answer calls after seven?"
8. Script review: a no-show problem worth about £1,000 a month; a "very important you reply" text and email sent
   24 hours before; then a GoHighLevel workflow that offers freed slots to people who visited the booking page but didn't book.
9. Sound human: spot the AI tells ("No painful migration you didn't need"), fix stop-start sentences, and Taylor
   reads a rewritten script word for word.
10. Save your skills and their versions; build cornerstone skills over time (2+ years and over 10K invested in theirs).
11. CTA: book a call for help building ICP research, a video-script skill, or confidence speaking on camera.

STRONGEST LINES FOR THUMBNAILS / TITLES (Taylor's own words)
- "AI doesn't know how to speak to humans."
- "Take the words out of their mouth."
- "This person has just read my mind."
- "No answer. Next clinic."
- "They still don't land the people you want them to land."
- "Diagnose before you write."

CHAPTERS (exact timestamps for the description)
0:00 Intro: why your Meta ads land the wrong people
0:49 Andromeda: the top-down and horizontal funnel
2:32 Research Radar: the AI research agent
4:21 The live ICP report

26:22 The rebooking workflow
28:27 Sound human: fixing AI scripts
33:10 Save your skills
35:47 Wrap-up and how we can help

VISUAL ASSETS AVAILABLE FOR THUMBNAILS
- Taylor on camera with a headset, in a rounded-square or circle frame with a brass border.
- On-screen graphics that already exist in the video:
  - the report's "#1 Growth-stage med spas · 88%" ranked card
  - the horizontal funnel
  - the objection donut (40/25/15%)
  - "AI doesn't know how to speak to humans." title (highlighted word: humans)
  - "Take the words out of their mouth."
  - "No answer. Next clinic." quote card
  - the AI vs You comparison
- Brand look:
  - cream ground #F5EAD8, ink #1C1A17, brass #8B6002 (bright brass #CB8E03), highlight #F6DF8C
  - fonts: Fraunces for headlines, Instrument Sans for body text, IBM Plex Mono for small labels
  - official logo mark only
  - red = problem, green = fix

RULES
- Don't name the client company from the report in the thumbnail, title or description. Say "a med spa client" instead.
- No invented results or numbers. Only use figures said in the video (88%, 40/25/15%, £1,000 a month, 90% done, 24 hours).
- Titles under 70 characters, with the main hook in the first 40. Thumbnail text 2–5 words, and it must not repeat the title.
- Description: hook in the first 2 lines; what they'll learn; the chapters above; the CTA (book a call, with the
  placeholder [BOOKING LINK]); 3–5 hashtags. Leave placeholders for any links you don't have.

OUTPUT

- the text on the image
- layout (Taylor's position, expression, graphic)

- why it earns the click
- which title it pairs with

Then rank your top 3 title + thumbnail pairs.
```
