Your AI agent crashed. Here's how it picks back up.

Updates happen and Claude Code or Codex restarts mid-run. A good harness doesn't start again. It reads 6 files and carries on:

interview.md: the nine questions and the answers
contract.md: what must stay true
plan.md: current step, next action
progress.md: evidence, blockers, live ledger
constraints.md: every requirement as a rule
features.json: every feature, exactly as needed
