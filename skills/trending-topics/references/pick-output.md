# The pick sheet

Pick mode ends with this sheet in the chat, then stops for Taylor's choice. Save a copy to the scratchpad as `picks-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and send it with SendUserFile.

## Template

```markdown
# Trending Topics: picks for week [YYYY-Www]

Research window: [start date] to [end date] (14 days).
Lane A: [n] chat sessions read, [n] work signals. Lane B: [n] creators, [n] in-window uploads.

## The five

### 1. [Working title]  ·  score [n]/50

**In 5 to 10 words:** [what the video teaches]

**Title options:**
- [title A]
- [title B]
- [title C, optional]

**Hook:** "[one opening line]"

**Our evidence (Lane A):**
- [session title or skill name]: [what we did, proof available] ([session or artifact link])

**Market evidence (Lane B):**
| Creator | Video | Views | Outlier | Age | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [name] | [title] | [views] | [ratio] | [days] | [url] |

**Our angle:** [why we beat the creators on this: real work, live demo, a different audience]

**Demo idea:** [one line]

**Score:** proof [n]×3 · heat [n]×2 · angle [n]×2 · demo [n] · fit [n] · fresh [n]

**Risks:** [confidential details, unverified sources, a competitor already covered it]

[repeat for 2 to 5]

## Market-only themes (no work of ours yet)
- [theme]: [creator evidence]. Would need: [what we'd have to build or do first].

## Recommended three
1. [title]: [one-line reason]
2. [title]: [one-line reason]
3. [title]: [one-line reason]

**How they link as a series:** [one line]

Pick 3 and I'll plan the week.
```

## Rules

- Exactly 5 topics, ranked by score. If fewer than 5 pass the gates, show what passed and say why the rest failed. Never pad.
- Every market claim has a URL and its date. Say "views rounded" for listing views.
- Our evidence names real sessions, skills or artifacts. If a link is private, say so.
- Do not reuse a topic already covered by a deck in `public/presentations.js` or by a week pack from the last 4 weeks, unless the new angle is clearly different. Say which.
