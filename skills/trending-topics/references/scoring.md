# Creator-first scoring

Our creators have already tested topics on a real audience. A breakout (a video that beat the creator's own normal) is that test passing. We score topics on that evidence alone. Our own work is not scored: it comes in as talking points after Taylor chooses (`chat-research.md`).

## Step 1: build candidates

1. Start from the **Core winners** table in `scan.md`: every core creator's long-form uploads at 1.4× or more, any date, plus the in-window uploads.
2. Group them into themes in `lane-b.md` (`creator-research.md`): same subject, or same promise and format, across creators.
3. A candidate is one theme plus our version of it: the promise they proved, aimed at Pecuna Factorem's audience (business owners, marketers, agency operators), with our searchable noun.

## Step 2: score each candidate 1 to 5

| Factor | Weight | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator proof | ×3 | Best upload on the theme at 5× or more | Nothing above 1.4× |
| Spread | ×2 | 4 or more core creators have a winner on it | One creator, one upload |
| Viewer promise | ×2 | A clear gain or risk for the viewer, a money skill, a time-box ("learn 97% in 19 minutes", "replace sales reps") | A tour of a tool, nothing at stake |
| Freshness | ×1 | A breakout inside the 14-day window | Winners all older than 3 months |
| Audience fit | ×1 | Our buyers need it to grow their business | Off-audience (gaming, gadgets, celebrity) |
| Open lane | ×1 | Our version is clearly different from every creator video on it, and we have not recorded it in 4 weeks | Saturated this fortnight, or we just did it |

Proof bands: 5 = 5× or more · 4 = 3× to 5× · 3 = 2× to 3× · 2 = 1.4× to 2× · 1 = under 1.4×. A single 100×-plus viral counts as 5 but never as a theme on its own.

Spread bands (core creators with a winner on the theme): 5 = 4 or more · 4 = 3 · 3 = 2 · 2 = 1 with a hit of 5× or more · 1 = 1 otherwise.

Total out of 50. Show the breakdown on the pick sheet.

## Hard gates (fail any, and it is not a pick)

- **No core creator winner:** at least one core upload on the theme at 1.4× or more, with its URL.
- **Secondary only:** a theme seen only on the app watchlist is not a pick.
- **No viewer promise:** if no title option promises the viewer something, rework it or drop it.
- **Off-audience:** model launches, gadgets and news only qualify if the angle is what it does for a business.
- **Duplicate:** we recorded or planned it in the last 4 weeks and the creators' angle is not new.

Having no work of ours on the topic is **not** a gate. Say what to do before recording instead.

## Tie-breaks

1. Higher creator proof.
2. More spread.
3. The fresher breakout.
4. The topic that pairs best with the other recommended picks as a series.

## Copying creators honestly

We borrow the topic, the promise and the format. We never copy their script, thumbnail or title word for word, never imply they endorse us, and name them only as commentary ("Sabri's most successful video this year...").
