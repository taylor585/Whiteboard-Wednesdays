# Hybrid scoring

Topics come from where our work (Lane A) meets what is working for creators (Lane B). A topic with heat but no work of ours is a market-only theme, not a pick.

## Step 1: build candidates

1. List every Lane A work signal (references/chat-research.md).
2. List every Lane B theme (references/creator-research.md).
3. Match them. A candidate is a work signal plus the theme it answers, for example:
   - "Harness Chassis Builder skill" + theme "AI harnesses / long-running agents" (Nick Saraev, AI HARNESS explainer).
   - "Meta ads ICP research pipeline" + theme "Meta ads post-Andromeda" (Sergio C).
4. Work signals with no matching theme can still be candidates (evergreen proof), but score low on heat.

## Step 2: score each candidate 1 to 5

| Factor | Weight | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Our proof | ×3 | Built and used in the window, with artifacts, numbers or a live demo | Mentioned once, nothing to show |
| Market heat | ×2 | Several creators in the window, an OUTLIER or HOT upload on the theme | No creator touched it |
| Our angle / gap | ×2 | We show something no creator showed (real client work, a working system, a different audience) | Same as an existing video, nothing new |
| Demo-ability | ×1 | Can be shown on screen end to end | Abstract, nothing to show |
| Audience fit | ×1 | Pecuna Factorem's buyers (business owners, marketers, agency operators) need it | Off-audience |
| Freshness | ×1 | New this fortnight and not covered by our decks or recent weeks | Already covered by us |

Total out of 50. Show the breakdown on the pick sheet.

## Hard gates (fail any, and it is not a pick)

- **No Lane A evidence:** list it under "Market-only themes" with what we would need to build first.
- **No URL for a market claim:** drop the claim or find the URL.
- **Depends on confidential client detail to make sense:** only a pick if it works with the client anonymised; flag it.
- **Duplicate:** an existing deck in `public/presentations.js` or a week pack from the last 4 weeks covers it, and the angle is not clearly new.

## Tie-breaks

1. Higher "Our proof".
2. The topic that pairs best with the other recommended picks as a series.
3. The fresher market signal (younger HOT or OUTLIER upload).

## Competitor overlap is not a fail

If a core creator already published on the topic (for example Nick Saraev's "AI HARNESS: An In-Depth Explanation"), that is heat, not a block. Score the angle honestly and put the overlap in the brief's "Angle check".
