# From creator research to trending topics

How the creator scan turns into a topic, an angle, a format and a title. This is the reasoning between the raw scan and the pick sheet.

## 1. The core idea

Our creators have already done the expensive work: they published, and their audience voted with views. A video that beat its creator's own normal (an outlier) shows a topic, a promise and a format that work right now. We piggyback on that.

```
Core winners (outlier 1.4×+, any date) ──┐
In-window uploads (fresh heat) ──────────┼──► proven theme ──► promise + format ──► our version ──► titles ──► pick
Packaging laws (research-findings §1) ───┘
                                                            (after Taylor picks)
                                                            our work ──► talking points ──► brief
```

Our own work is not part of the pick. It is added after Taylor chooses, as talking points, stories and demos for the brief.

## 2. Step by step

1. **Read the Core winners table** in `scan.md`. Note each creator's top breakouts and what they share.
2. **Group into proven themes** (`creator-research.md`) in `lane-b.md`. Group two ways:
   - **by subject:** Meta ads, sales, Grok, landing pages, the new era of AI;
   - **by promise and format:** "Learn 97% of X in N minutes", "Give me N minutes and I'll...", "I tested...", "on a low budget", "what I'd learn instead", "the top 0.01%".
   A format that wins for 3 creators on different subjects is a theme in its own right.
3. **Write the promise they proved** in one line, in the viewer's words: "learn the whole of Meta ads in under 20 minutes"; "get results without a big budget".
4. **Write our version:** the same promise for our audience, with our searchable noun (Claude Code, AI, AI agents, Meta ads with AI). Change the subject or the twist, never just the wording.
5. **Pick the format** from the packaging laws (section 3), starting from the format the creators proved.
6. **Score** with `scoring.md` and apply the hard gates.
7. **Write the pick** (`pick-output.md`), including title options from `headlines.md` that borrow the proven formula.

## 3. Applying the packaging laws to topic choice

| Law | What it means for choosing and framing a topic |
|---|---|
| Stakes beat demos (5) | Frame the topic around a gain or a risk to the viewer: "replace sales reps", "on a low budget". A plain tour of a tool is weak. |
| Time-box on money skills (3) | "Learn 97% of X in N minutes" is the strongest format on Sabri's and Daniel Priestley's channels. Use it for marketing, sales and ads topics. |
| Long courses win (4) | Nick's 6-hour course and Riley's 2-hour course both broke out. A topic that can become a complete course is worth recording as one. |
| "I tested" is portable (7) | If the creators' winner is a test, ours is a test on business work. |
| Fear works about the viewer's path (6) | Frame risk around the viewer's work ("sales reps will be replaced"), never "don't use this new model". |
| Formulas wear out (10) | If several creators used the formula this fortnight, use a different one from `headlines.md` §3 for one of the A/B titles. |
| News raises the floor (11) | A launch theme qualifies when the angle is what it does for a business, not the launch itself. |
| Search and browse (1) | The topic must have a searchable noun as well as a hook. |

## 4. Reading heat honestly

- **Several creators, strong breakouts:** top pick.
- **One creator, huge breakout, nobody else:** a pick only if the promise travels to our audience. Name the single source.
- **One creator, one 100×-plus viral:** an anomaly. Use it as heat for a theme that other creators also prove, never alone.
- **Breakouts older than 3 months, nothing fresh:** still a pick if the format is evergreen (courses, sales, Meta ads), with lower freshness.
- **Off-niche viral theme:** ignore unless it maps to Pecuna Factorem's buyers.
- **Every creator posted on it this fortnight:** proven but crowded. Find a different twist or format, and score open lane honestly.

## 6. Turning a pick into a brief

Once Taylor chooses, build the brief with `brief-format.md`. The research feeds four places:
- **Talking points:** from our own work (`chat-research.md`), found only now.
- **READ BEFORE YOU RECORD, "Angle check":** the creators' winning videos and how ours differs.
- **PACKAGING:** the A/B titles, thumbnail lines and description, from `headlines.md` and `descriptions.md`, borrowing the proven formula.
- **Act 3 and Verify first:** any number or claim the research cannot back stays blank or flagged.
