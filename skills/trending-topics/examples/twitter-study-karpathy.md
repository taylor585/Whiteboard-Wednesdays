## The useful information

1. **He builds tools for himself, fast.** HackerTube took about 2 hours, TristanTube 3 minutes, and the browser extension one sentence. None of them is a product. Each fixes something that annoyed him.
2. **He fixes the feed instead of putting up with it.** He disliked YouTube's recommendations, so he used other people's judgement (20 years of Hacker News comments, a friend's list) to build his own.
3. **He gives the exact prompt.** The 3 Nov reply is a one-sentence Claude Code prompt that anyone could copy.
4. **The work is mostly filtering.** HackerTube's hard part was cutting 43 million comments down to 6,454 videos. The website is the easy part.
5. **The AI-understanding funnel (11 Oct).** 75% of people have barely used AI, and 0.2% are watching agents do weeks of work from one prompt. Business owners sit in the gap between the two, and that is our whole audience.
6. **A cheap test of what a model knows (2 Oct).** Ask it one simple question thousands of times and plot the answers. Replies pointed out that always answering "water" scores about 70%, so a high score can mislead. That's a lesson on testing AI honestly.
7. **He's backing slower frontier AI (12 Sep).** His most-viewed post here, at about 966K views.
