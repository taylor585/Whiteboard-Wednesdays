# Publish jobs: what the Claude Code session does

Taylor pastes one Drive folder link (or file links) on **Publish** and presses **Generate Variety Pack**.
The app saves `jobs/<id>` in its own artifact database (https://claude.ai/artifact/WCzdc2u9RBzuzqz5JhHwea) and starts a Claude Code session in the cloud by itself, through Taylor's Claude Code Remote connector (3 November 2026).
That session's first message is `Run Publish job <id>`. Every later session starts the same way, by itself, when Taylor presses a button at a stop, and its line names the stop it continues from:

| Taylor presses | Line | The session |
|---|---|---|
| Continue (after his Variety Pack picks) | `Continue Publish job <id> from pack` | builds the pilot, stops on `pilot` (or runs a redo round and stops on `pack`) |
| Approve the pilot, or Change something | `Continue Publish job <id> from pilot` | renders the YouTube master and the LinkedIn cut and stops on `review` (or changes the pilot and stops on `pilot`) |
| Approve both, or Change something | `Continue Publish job <id> from review` | makes sixteen draft thumbnails and the posts and stops on `thumbs` (or on `ask` when the booking link or the buyer is missing), or makes the changes and stops on `review` again |
| Continue on `ask` (after adding the booking link or the buyer) | `Continue Publish job <id> from review` | makes the sixteen draft thumbnails and the posts from the approved cuts and stops on `thumbs` |
| Polish my picks (after picking thumbnail numbers on `thumbs`) | `Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails` | polishes only the thumbnails Taylor picked, makes their finished sizes and the feed mock, and stops on `posts` (7 November 2026: Thumbnail Tracker 4.0.0's polish step) |

Taylor's picks on `posts` start nothing: he picks one post per platform (his thumbnail is already the polished pick), presses **Ready to post**, and the app sets `final` itself. Posting stays by hand (Taylor's decision 8), in this order: YouTube first, then its link in the app so the LinkedIn first comment and the X reply carry the real link, then LinkedIn, then X, then the reels.

**Until the app shows the thumbnails stop** (the Video lane adds the button; the fields are under "The job document"), Taylor can send the line in a chat with his numbers, for example `Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails 7, 12 for LinkedIn`. Only then does the session write the thumbnail picks itself (see "Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails").

`Continue Publish job <id>` with no stop named (jobs from before 5 November 2026) means `from pack`.
Each session is new and runs in a new container: it starts by restoring the **work set** (below), never by transcribing or planning again.
If the connector is refused, Taylor sends the same line in a chat. Either way, follow this file exactly.

## The job document

- `title`, `brief`: what the video is about, in Taylor's words. `titleBy: "taylor"` when he typed or confirmed it; null when he pasted a folder and left the title empty (it then comes from the folder name, and he confirms it on Check the files).
- `source`: `{kind: "folder", folderId, url}` when Taylor pasted one Drive folder, or `{kind: "files"}` for file links.
- `sources`: the files, each `{url, driveId, role: "main" | "reel" | "skip", part, roleBy, check, name, kind, decision, fileDecision, recorded, proposedRole, reason, probe, confirmedAt}`. `fileDecision` is the classifier's answer from the file alone, without the role it was pasted with, and is what the Check the files screen preselects from: only a file whose `fileDecision` is `accept` gets a role preselected; every other file starts on Choose. `recorded` is the camera's recording date (or null), and `proposedRole` the role the files themselves give it (see "Check the files"). `part` (1..n) is only on the parts of a main recorded in parts (see "A main recording in parts"); a single main has none. For a folder job it starts empty and the Check the files stage fills it. For file links it starts with the role Taylor chose and `check: "pending"`. There is exactly one main recording (landscape, 32:9 or 16:9; the house recording is 3840x1080, screen plus camera): one file, or its parts. `reel` files are phone reel recordings (upright, 9:16 once the rotation flag is applied), at most five. `skip` files are left out.
- `reelTakes`: the `driveId`s of the reel recordings. They wait for the reel winners (see "Main recording and reel recordings").
- `folderName`, `checkAt`, `checkNote`, `checkedAt`: written by Check the files (the first three) and by Taylor's Confirm (`checkedAt`).
- `topicId`: the saved topic this video belongs to, or null if Taylor did not link one.
- `startedAt`: when Taylor pressed Generate. `session: {id, url, step, requestedAt, startedAt}`: when the app asked for the session and when it opened. Do not change these.
- `skills`: the skill chosen for each job in Settings. Use **exactly these skills**, by these names, with the Skill tool:
  - `publishEdit` (default `suby-shots-split-screen-katana`, Suby Shots - Split Screen Katana 5.1.0, since 10 November 2026; `suby-shots-with-katana` 5.0 from 4 November): the edit and its Variety Pack. Split Screen Katana is With Katana 5.0 kept whole plus master rule 10 (reels are split screen, see "Reels jobs"), so the YouTube master, the LinkedIn cut, the Variety Pack and master rules 1 to 9 are exactly as before. The pack's page name is whatever that skill's `scripts/variety-pack/build.py` writes: **Katana Kutz - Variety Pack** for Split Screen Katana 5.1 and With Katana 5.0, **Suby Shots - Variety Pack** for Master Poster. Below, "the pack" means that page.
  - `publishThumbs` (for example `thumbnail-tracker-16-variations`): thumbnails.
  - `publishPosts` (for example `performance-poster`): titles, descriptions and posts.
  If a field is missing, use the default from `public/settings.js`. Never swap in a different skill or recreate one from memory.
- `status`: `queued` → `starting` → `running` (→ `check` → `starting` → `running`) (→ `found`) → `pack` → `finishing` → `pilot` → `finishing` → `review` → `finishing` (→ `ask` → `finishing`) → `thumbs` → `finishing` → `posts` → `final`. `failed` with `error` if it cannot finish. The app sets `starting` and `finishing` itself, only after the session has opened; `session.step` then says which stop it continues from (`finals` is from the pack, then `pilot`, then `review`, then `thumbs`, whose line is `Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails`).
- The stops after the pack (Phase 5, 4 November 2026; Taylor's decision 2: keep the pilot stop, then approve the YouTube master and the LinkedIn cut together, in one stop):
  - `work: {url, archive, manifest, files: [{path, bytes}], bytes, stage, savedAt}`: the saved work set (see "The work set"). Written by the session only.
  - `reviewUrl`: the job's private review page, a claude.ai artifact link. One per job, made at the pilot and updated in place after that.
  - `pilot: {url, seconds, round, renderedAt, detail}`: written when the pilot is ready. `url` is the review page.
  - `cuts: {url, round, renderedAt, master: {label, seconds, detail, url?}, linkedin: {label, seconds, detail, url?}}`: written when the master and the LinkedIn cut are ready.
  - `approvals: {pilot, review}`: Taylor's answer at each stop, written by the app together with the session it starts: `{decision: "approved" | "changes", note, round, at}`. The session reads it and never changes it. An answer is current when its `at` is later than the `renderedAt` it answers.
  - `finalNote`: one sentence on the finished job, shown above `finals`.
- Thumbnails and posts (Phase 7, 4 November 2026; Taylor's decisions 7 and 8):
  - `buyer` (`"A"` wants AI built, `"B"` buys paid ads, or null), `buyerBy` (`"topic"` or `"taylor"`) and `bookingLink` (the https link saved once in Settings as `settings/links`): written by the app with **Approve both** and with Continue on `ask`. The buyer comes from the topic's `buyer`, and Taylor can change it for this video. The session reads these three and never changes them.
  - `ask: {missing: ["booking" | "buyer"], note, at}`: written by the session when it stops on `ask` (see "Continue from review").
  - `pack: {note, thumbRound, thumbFinals, platforms: [{key: "youtube" | "linkedin" | "x", label, postLabel, note, thumbnails: [{id, label, tag, size, url, file}], posts: [{title, body, firstComment, check, checker: {fails, warns, chars, exceptions}}]}]}`: written by the session, first on `thumbs` (with `thumbRound` and the posts, and `thumbnails: []` on each platform) and then on `posts` (the polished picks in `thumbnails`, and `thumbFinals`). The app's PB-A blocks read exactly this.
  - **The X platform** (10 November 2026; `key: "x"`, `label: "X"`, `postLabel: "Post"`, always the third platform, `thumbnails: []` always, and a `note` saying what to attach). Its two `posts` have their own shape (see "The X post"):
    ```text
    {kind: "single" | "thread",
     body:    <the first post: the hook, no link>,
     replies: [<each post after it, in order>],      single: exactly one, the reply with [YOUTUBE LINK]
                                                     thread: the rest of the thread (4 to 6 posts in all), [YOUTUBE LINK] in the last
     check:   "PASS · single post · 261 of 280 · 0 hashtags · 0 WARN",
     checker: {fails: 0, warns, chars: <the longest post, as X counts it>, counts: [<every post>], posts, hashtags, exceptions: []}}
    ```
    No `title` and no `firstComment`. Taylor's pick is `picks["x.post"]` (`"v1"` the single, `"v2"` the thread), written by the app like the others. The app counts every post again itself (`public/x-count.js`) and refuses one over 280.
  - `pack.thumbRound` (7 November 2026, Thumbnail Tracker 4.0.0; written by the session on `thumbs`, never changed after Taylor picks):
    ```text
    {skill: "thumbnail-tracker-16-variations", version: "4.0.0", round: 1, n: 16, renderedAt,
     sheet:  <the numbered contact sheet's image file URL>,          thumbs/contact-sheet.jpg
     strip:  <the 168 px colour + greyscale strip's image file URL>,  thumbs/check-168px.png
     check:  {fails: 0, warns, line: "Variety: 16 thumbnails, 0 FAIL, 1 WARN", history: <job id or null>},
     variations: [{n, id: "t<n>", words, layout, ground, hero, font, accent, emoji: true | false,
                   size: "1280×720", url, file}]}                     n = 1..16, the contact-sheet number
    ```
    Every `url`, `file`, `sheet` and `strip` is an **image file URL**: the address a `scope: "files"` listing of the review page gives for that published file. Never a page anchor (`…/artifact/<id>#thumb-youtube-1`): the app's `<img>` cannot load one, and the 6 November job showed "Preview not available here" on every thumbnail. `id` is always `t` plus the contact-sheet number, so "I want 7" is pick `t7`.
  - `pack.thumbFinals` (written by the session on `posts`): `{polishedAt, feedMock: <image file URL>, files: [{id: "t<n>", n, size: "1280×720" | "1920×1080" | "1080×1350", use: "YouTube thumbnail" | "LinkedIn video cover" | "4:5 post image", url, file, drive?: {driveId, url}}]}`. `pack.platforms[].thumbnails` keeps its shape and holds only the polished picks: YouTube the 1280×720 of `picks["thumbs.youtube"]` (and of `picks["thumbs.ab"]`, the Test & Compare second, when picked), LinkedIn the 1920×1080 of `picks["thumbs.linkedin"]`; their `id`s are `t<n>`.
  - `picks: {"thumbs.youtube": "t<n>", "thumbs.linkedin": "t<n>", "thumbs.ab": "t<n>" | null, "<key>.thumb": <thumbnail id>, "<key>.post": "v1" | "v2"}`, `thumbsPickedAt`, `readyAt`, `youtubeUrl`: written by the app only (the one exception: the chat line on `thumbs`, see "Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails", which also writes `thumbsPickedBy: "chat"`). `thumbs.linkedin` defaults to the YouTube number; `thumbs.ab` is optional and must be on a different ground from `thumbs.youtube`. **Polish my picks** writes the three `thumbs.*` keys and `thumbsPickedAt`, sets `status: "finishing"` and `session.step: "thumbs"`, and starts `Continue Publish job <id> from thumbnails`. On `posts` the app preselects `<key>.thumb` when a platform has one thumbnail. `youtubeUrl` is the video's link once Taylor has posted it by hand; the app puts it into the LinkedIn first comment and the X reply (the last post of an X thread).
  - `cuts.master.full`, `cuts.linkedin.full` (written by the session, only when the Drive upload key is set: see "Full-quality delivery"): `{driveId, url, name, bytes, width, height, seconds, round, uploadedAt}`, the full-quality file of that round in Taylor's Drive.
  - `finals[]` entries carry `quality: "full" | "review"`: `full` is the file to upload (on Drive), `review` the review copy on the private review page (720p for the master), never to be uploaded.
- Earlier work (Phase 3, 4 November 2026):
  - `existing: [{url, title, kind, round, decisions, why, createdAt}]` and `foundNote`: written by **Look for earlier work** when it stops on `found`. `kind` is `variety`, `katana` or `reels`.
  - `existingSeen`: links Taylor was already shown in the app and chose to start fresh over (pack links, or `jobs/<id>`). Ignore these when looking.
  - `skipExisting: true`: Taylor pressed Start fresh anyway on `found`. Skip Look for earlier work.
  - `reusedFrom: {url, title, pack?, at}`: Taylor pressed Use this pack, so `packUrl` is that earlier pack and nothing is rebuilt.
  - `packRecord`: the run record of the pack this job published (see Run step 7).
- `stages`: the progress list the Publish screen shows: `[{name, detail, state: "wait" | "run" | "done" | "failed", startedAt, endedAt}]`. Write it as you go; never write a stage you have not done.
- **Plain words for Taylor (7 November 2026).** Every `name`, `detail`, `checkNote`, `foundNote`, `note`, `error`, `finalNote` and `*.detail` line is shown to Taylor, so write it as one plain sentence about what happened to his video, for example "Sound levelled and in sync with the picture", "Saved your working files so the next step starts where this one stopped", "Words on screen checked: nothing overlaps or runs off the edge". Never put these in them: LUFS, dBTP, true peak, sha256, base64, tar, h264, HEVC, AAC, CRF, Cycles, youtube_guard, reels.py, build.py, check_post.py, variety_check.py, ffmpeg, ffprobe, work.tar.gz, guard registry, file paths or style ids (`pr-ribbon`). The numbers and tool names go only in the QA files (`jobs/<id>/qa/*.json`, kept in the work set), which are for checking, not for Taylor.
- **Stage times are real.** Take `startedAt` from `date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ` at the moment the stage starts, and `endedAt` the same way at the moment it ends. Never round, estimate or backfill a time, and never write a time from before this session opened.
- The Publish screen shows "Claude Code has not reported back" when a job stays on `starting` for 10 minutes, or on `running` or `finishing` with no new stage time for 90 minutes. Start or end a stage at least that often; split a long render into stages if you need to.
- **Taylor can stop a job.** His Stop button writes `status: "failed"`, `error: "Stopped by Taylor"`. Re-read the job before each stage; if it says that, stop at once and write nothing more.

Write to the job with ArtifactData, pinned to the version you read (`if_version`). Never touch other jobs, topics, creator notes or settings.

## The packs index (`packs/<id>`)

One document per review pack ever published for Taylor's videos, in the app's database, so a recording is never packed twice. The id is the pack's artifact id (the last part of its claude.ai link).

`{url, title, kind: "variety" | "katana" | "reels", skill, round, driveIds, folderId, seconds, topicId, jobId, decisions, createdAt, updatedAt, by: "session" | "backfill"}`

- `title` is the video's name as the pack shows it; `driveIds` the main recording's Drive file ids (every part, in part order); `seconds` its measured length (the joined length for a main in parts) (null when not measured); `decisions` the collection the pack saves picks in (`decisions` for a Variety Pack, `katana` for a Katana moments page, `winners` or `picks` for reel pages, null when the page saves only in the browser).
- The app reads it for its free check before Generate. Sessions add a document for every pack they publish (Run step 7), and for an earlier pack they find that is missing (Look for earlier work). Add documents only: never change or delete another pack's document, and never touch any pack's `decisions`.

## Main recording and reel recordings (master rule 4)

- Only the one source with `role: "main"` goes to the `publishEdit` skill as the recording for YouTube and LinkedIn. Its kind is `youtube-recording`, or Taylor confirmed it on Check the files.
- Never use a `reel` file as the main, and never give a reel file to the YouTube or LinkedIn edit.
- Never cut a reel, Short or Instagram or Facebook video from the main recording. The only material from it that may reach a reel is the skill's own silent, face-free screen-share tool (on Path B, the B-roll over Taylor's phone take: master rule 4's only exception, Taylor's decision 9).
- Reel recordings (`reelTakes`) are used only after Taylor picks the reel winners. Before one is used as a reel's face or voice, it must pass the skill's `youtube_guard check-media` (and `check-words` after transcription).
- `skip` files are never downloaded or used.

## A main recording in parts (6 November 2026)

Taylor sometimes records the main video in several parts (for example "Top 50 - Part 1.mp4", "Part 2", "Part 3", all 3840x1080 with sound). They are **one** main recording:
- On the job, every part is a source with `role: "main"` and `part` 1..n, the order they are joined (Taylor can reorder or leave out a part on Check the files). A job with one main has no `part` and nothing below changes for it.
- Wherever this file says "the main recording", "the main `driveId`" or `role: "main"`, read: every part, joined in `part` order into one file. Never give a single part to any skill.
- **Join before anything else** (Run step 3, and every later session that downloads the main recording):
  1. Download every part (the same `curl` line, one file each, `part1.mp4` … `partN.mp4`) and `ffprobe` each: video codec, width, height, pixel format, frame rate and time base; audio codec, sample rate and channels.
  2. If every part's streams match, join without re-encoding:
     `for f in part*.mp4; do printf "file '%s'\n" "$PWD/$f"; done > parts.txt` (in part order), then
     `ffmpeg -hide_banner -f concat -safe 0 -i parts.txt -map 0:v:0 -map 0:a:0 -c copy -movflags +faststart main.mp4`.
  3. If any stream differs, re-encode once to part 1's settings: `ffmpeg -i part1.mp4 … -i partN.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v:0][0:a:0]…[N-1:v:0][N-1:a:0]concat=n=N:v=1:a=1[v][a]" -map "[v]" -map "[a]" -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -preset medium -pix_fmt yuv420p -r <part 1 frame rate> -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 -movflags +faststart main.mp4`. Parts of different picture sizes never get here: Check the files refuses them.
  4. Check the joined file: it has one video and one audio stream, its size is part 1's, and its length equals the sum of the parts' lengths (within 0.5 s plus 0.1 s per part). Otherwise fail the job: "The parts of the main recording did not join cleanly (joined <x> s, parts <y> s)."
  5. From then on `main.mp4` is the main recording everywhere: transcript, map and plan times, the guard's `register-source`, the pack, the pilot, the master and the LinkedIn cut are all on the joined timeline. Name it in the stage detail ("3 parts joined: 21:17").
- **Records use every part:** `packs/<id>` `driveIds` lists every part id in order and `seconds` is the joined length; the work-set manifest's `source` is `{driveId (part 1), driveIds, bytes (all parts), seconds (joined)}`; Look for earlier work and the work set treat any part id as this recording.
- The joined file is never saved (it is as large as the parts): a later session downloads the parts and joins them again, which takes a minute or two.

## Check the files (the first stage of every Run)

This stage only reads a few MB of each file. It never downloads a full file and never starts the edit.

1. Skip this stage when every source has `confirmedAt` (Taylor pressed Confirm and continue). Go straight to Run step 2.
2. **Folder job** (`source.kind: "folder"`, `sources` empty): run
   `python3 -I scripts/classify-video.py --drive-folder <folderId> > check.json`.
   It lists the folder from `https://drive.google.com/embeddedfolderview?id=<folderId>` (no key) and classifies every file.
   - Copy its `sources` to the job, each with `check: "done"`, and `folderName`. Copy `fileDecision` and `recorded` with the rest.
   - Its roles are a proposal only. Exactly one landscape recording with sound, 5 minutes or longer, becomes `main`, or (6 November 2026) several landscape recordings with sound whose names carry part numbers ("Part 1", "Pt 2", "- 3", "(2)", "take 2"; the words before the number may differ slightly, such as Make and Create) and that share one picture size become one `main` in parts, with `part` 1..n in number order, judged on their total length, and `accept`. Its `main` is `{parts: [driveId in part order], seconds}`. Phone recordings it is sure of become `reel`. Everything else is `skip`. When several files could be the main, or the parts are unclear (a missing or repeated number, mixed sizes, a copy of another part, an unnumbered recording beside them, names that differ), none is chosen and each is `ask`.
   - **What it is sure of (classification audit, 7 November 2026; never silently wrong: a file is either right or asked).**
     - **Only camera metadata proves a phone take:** an iPhone or iPad model tag, Android tags, or HDR together with a rotation flag. A phone-style name (IMG_1234), HEVC in a .mov or a rotation flag alone can be copied onto any file, so an upright file with only those is `ask` and its role is left empty (Taylor chooses).
     - **The finished-reel check runs first:** an upright 1080×1920 H.264 BT.709 30 fps file with sound and no camera metadata is the house finished reel, an output (`reject`), whatever its name. Over 60 s, or when its length cannot be read, it is asked instead (a phone take converted to H.264, or an export). The 1080×1350 house spec is the 4:5 post, an output.
     - **The finished-master guard:** a 1920×1080 30 fps house render over 15 minutes is a finished YouTube master export, not the raw recording: asked.
     - **The length check:** a header length that does not fit the file size (more than 250 Mbit/s, or a fragmented MP4 such as OBS "Hybrid MP4") is never trusted: the file is asked, and Run step 3 measures it after the download.
     - **The recording date:** a phone take recorded more than 21 days before today is asked ("is it a take for this video?"), never proposed as a reel.
     - **A take always stops once:** a proposed reel beside the main recording adds the problem "phone take(s) beside the main recording: Taylor confirms before the reels start (decision 10)", so `needsCheck` is true (step 4).
   - If the folder cannot be listed, fail the job: "The folder is not shared as Anyone with the link, or it is empty."
3. **File links** (`source.kind: "files"`): check every source in **one** call, so file links get the same proposal as a folder (7 November 2026):
   `python3 -I scripts/classify-video.py --drive-files <driveId>:<role>[:<part>],<driveId>:<role>[:<part>],…`
   (in Taylor's order; `<part>` only on the parts of a main in parts, for example `id1:main:1,id2:main:2,id3:reel`). It classifies each file on its own (`fileDecision`), runs the folder's `propose()` over them (one main or its parts, the take rules and the 21-day recording date above), and keeps the role Taylor pasted (`pastedRole`) beside the role the files give (`proposedRole`). A main in parts is judged in Taylor's part order on the total length, and asked when the parts are not one size or one is a copy of another. A file pasted as `skip` is left out of the proposal. `decision` is the worse of `fileDecision` and the file judged against its pasted role, so a pasted role the files do not support is never `accept`.
   For each source write `kind`, `decision`, `fileDecision`, `recorded`, `proposedRole`, `reason` (joined with "; "), `probe` (`width`, `height` after rotation, `rotation`, `band`, `seconds`, `audio`) and `check: "done"`. Keep Taylor's `role` and `part`. (`--drive <driveId> --role <role> --json` for one file and `--main-parts` still work, for the Reels lane and older jobs.)
   The kind is the file's real shape, whatever role it was pasted with: Taylor often pastes the phone take first, so it arrives as `role: "main"` (for example IMG_3094.MOV, 1920x1080 HEVC with rotation -90, shows upright 1080x1920, is a `phone-recording`; the main 1V9L7Iojg27yDidtlCyUNmCjZTHW412AS, 3840x1080, 38:13, is a `youtube-recording`). Keep Taylor's role on the job and let the mismatch stop on `check`: the Check the files screen offers each file the role its kind gives it (a `youtube-recording` is the main, a `phone-recording` a reel), so one Confirm sets both and starts Path B.
4. Carry on to Run step 2 only when all of these hold:
   - every file is `accept` (its `decision`, which already includes `fileDecision`);
   - there is exactly one main recording and it is a `youtube-recording`: one source with `role: "main"`, or its parts, each a `youtube-recording` with `part` numbers running 1 to n with no gap or repeat, all one picture size;
   - every `reel` is a `phone-recording` that is `accept`, and there are at most five;
   - `titleBy` is `"taylor"`;
   - there is no `reel` file (6 November 2026, Taylor's decision 10). A phone take beside the main recording always stops here once, even when nothing else is unclear, because Taylor's **Confirm and continue** is what starts its reels (Path B, see "Reels jobs"). Say so in the `checkNote`, for example "Your phone take IMG_3097.MOV will be cut into reels: confirm the files."
5. Otherwise stop here. Write `status: "check"`, `checkAt` (the real time), a one-sentence `checkNote` saying what Taylor must decide (for example "Two files could be the main recording: choose one."), the finished stage, and end the session.
   The Publish screen then shows each file's real shape, length and reason, the title from the folder name matched to his booked topic, and a role per file. Taylor presses **Confirm and continue**, which starts a fresh session with `Run Publish job <id>`.
   When the confirmed files hold one or more phone takes (`role: "reel"`, `kind: "phone-recording"`) beside the main recording, the same press also creates the Path B reel job `reels/<id>` (`auto: true`, `jobId` this job) and starts its own session with `Run Reels job <id>` (see "Reels jobs", "Path B"). This job is never written for it. If the connector refuses the Video session, the reel job waits on `queued` and starts with this job's next session (Try again), or from Start now on the Reels lane.

## Look for earlier work (the second stage of every Run, before any download)

The "Thinking With AI" job of 3 November rebuilt a pack for a recording that already had one from a chat (*How to think*, 41 approvals, master and LinkedIn cut built). This stage stops that. It only reads: it never publishes, and never writes to another artifact or its `decisions`.

1. Skip this stage when `skipExisting` is true, or when the job already has `packUrl` (Taylor chose Use this pack: see Run step 2).
2. Read the `packs` collection and the other `jobs` (ArtifactData `list`, `query.limit: 1000`). Leave out anything in `existingSeen`, failed jobs with no `packUrl`, and this job.
3. A pack or job is **the same recording** when any of these holds:
   - its `driveIds` (or a job's `sources[].driveId`) include this job's main `driveId` (any part's, for a main in parts), or its `folderId` is this job's `source.folderId`;

## Text fit check (7 November 2026, after the "How To Think With AI" master)

Taylor found, in a master he had approved through Claude, a compass label written across another label and three arrow phrases running off the right edge. Nothing caught them: the QA only measured sound, sync, black frames and banding, the pilot covered the first 77 seconds, and the Variety Pack showed the compass with shorter sample words. This check is the gate that stops it happening again. It is not optional and nothing overrides it.

**What it does.** This repository holds `scripts/text-fit-probe.tsx` (a Remotion entry that renders one beat through the skill's own `EpisodeBeat` and measures every visible line of text in the browser) and `scripts/text-fit-check.mts` (renders every beat in `src/v3/episode.ts` at two settled frames: two thirds in, and the last frame before the exit motion). It fails a beat when:
- **overlap:** a line of text sits on top of another line from a different element (more than 60 px², after allowing for ascenders and descenders);
- **off-frame:** any part of a line leaves the 1920 × 1080 frame;
- **clipped:** a line is cut off by the panel, pill or card that holds it (truncated mid-word, or spilling past a rounded panel).
It warns, without failing, when text sits inside the outer 40 px of the frame. Giant outline numerals (transparent fill with a stroke, such as the chapter numbers) and anything marked `data-textfit="decor"` are decoration and are skipped; mark a new decorative layer the same way, never a label.

**How to run it**, inside the skill working copy after the work set is restored (the main recording is not needed: the check only measures text):
```
mkdir -p src/textfit && cp <this repository>/scripts/text-fit-probe.tsx src/textfit/TextFit.tsx && cp <this repository>/scripts/text-fit-check.mts src/textfit/check.mts
npx tsx src/textfit/check.mts --out jobs/<id>/qa/text-fit.json --shots jobs/<id>/qa/text-fit     # all beats; --ids a,b for some
```
The main recording is not needed: when the live screen, the camera or a push shot's clean clip is missing, the check puts a plain grey stand-in in its place while it runs and removes it again at the end (real files are never touched). Keep `qa/text-fit.json` in the work set.

**When it runs.**
1. **Before the pilot** ("from pack" step 4): straight after the beats are locked to the approved styles, on every beat of the whole episode, not just the pilot's. Fix every failure before anything is rendered.
2. **Before the master** ("from pilot" step 4) and after every change round ("from pilot" step 3, "from review" step 2): on every beat, again. A master or a LinkedIn cut is never published to the review page with a failing beat.
3. **In the Variety Pack** (Run step 6): every overlay and full-screen candidate is rendered with this video's real words at the moment it would be used, never sample text, and the longest real label of each design is the one shown. Run the check on the pack's beats before `build.py`.

**How to fix a failure.** Fix the design, not just the words: a label belongs at the end of its arrow and grows away from the centre (a left label is right-aligned so it ends at the arrow tip), long phrases wrap or step down in size inside a width that fits the frame, a pill or lower third grows to its text or wraps it instead of cutting it off, and wrapped lines in a list keep a full line gap. Shorten the words only when Taylor's own words allow it. Re-run until it passes, and write the result in the stage detail in plain words ("Words on screen checked in all 110 moments: nothing overlaps, runs off the edge or is cut off"); the beat and frame counts go in `qa/text-fit.json`.

**The review stops show it.** Put the result in the `pilot.detail` and `cuts.master.detail` lines, and never write "QA passes" for a master whose text fit check has not passed.
