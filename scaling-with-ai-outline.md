## The one idea

Don't use AI to scale a guess. Use it to run lots of cheap micro tests wide (horizontal), find the one that wins (the pot of gold), then use AI to drill into that winner and scale it deep (vertical). Then go wide again from the new base.

## Simplicity passes (on this outline)

1. **Meaning:** one claim throughout: test wide, then scale deep. Dropped a separate "why AI matters" section; slide 08 carries that in one number.
2. **Structure:** the three test types sit on one slide (06) instead of three; the full subject lines get their own panel (07) because the real wording is the proof.
3. **Visual:** the core idea is one shape, a T (wide bar, deep drill), introduced on 02, used again on 15 and repeated as a loop on 18.
4. **Sequence:** every phase goes framing, real example, method, result. Each method slide (09, 16) follows the thing it explains.
5. **Audience:** headlines are three to five words. Slides 08 and 13 are the first to cut if the deck runs long or the evidence is thin.
